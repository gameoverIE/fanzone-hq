The problem we solve

Ibiza is the hardest premium market in the world to enter. That is exactly the point.

Every product crosses water. The season is compressed into six months. Alcohol is wrapped in excise law. And the best venues only buy from people they trust. Handled badly, each barrier keeps a great brand off the island. Handled by us, each one becomes the reason to work with PØRTAL.

01 — THE WATER

It's an island

Everything arrives by sea or air. We consolidate many brands into one tracked, cross-border shipment — friction becomes a service.

02 — THE SEASON

Six brutal months

Demand explodes May–October. Being organised, stocked and trusted before the window opens wins disproportionately.

03 — THE EXCISE

Duty & IVA

Alcohol routes through bonded warehousing and the EU's EMCS system. We carry the compliance so your brand never touches a customs form.

04 — THE TRUST

The rooms that matter

The best venues don't buy from strangers. They buy from people they know — a wall we've spent a decade on the right side of.

The model

One platform, four layers — built to move product and prove brands.

PØRTAL is a digital marketplace combined with an on-island route-to-market service. Brands list and test; venues, hotels, festivals and shops order from many brands at once, on a single invoice. In the middle, we run the parts that make entering Ibiza alone almost impossible.

LAYER 01

The Storefront

A clean, fast platform where venues browse every listed brand, filter by category and order in a few taps. Brands get a dashboard to list products, set pricing and see exactly where they're selling.

LAYER 02

The Fiscal Engine

Every product carries a compliance class that sets its IVA, excise treatment and age-verification automatically. Alcohol routes through bonded warehousing and EMCS; wellness and water ship simply. Legal and scalable across every category.

LAYER 03

The Logistics Rail

Orders from multiple brands consolidate into one shipment, cross into Ibiza, and track live from warehouse to ferry to venue — every piece of paperwork attached. The brand never touches a customs form.

LAYER 04

The In-Venue Edge

Placement, sampling and smart-vending inside the venues we already work with — selling directly to the end customer and feeding back proprietary data on what sells, where and when.

★ The signature service

The Lab

Prove Ibiza before you commit a single euro to a full launch.

You can't know whether the island will take to your product without spending a lot of money to find out — and most brands can't afford to find out the hard way. The Lab removes that risk. We place a selected brand's product, free, in a defined set of the right venues, for a fixed window, and measure exactly what happens: sell-through, reorder interest, staff and customer response, and where it performs best.

For the brand, it's real proof in real venues — the difference between a gamble and a decision. Succeed here, and “Tested in The Lab” becomes a credential the island recognises.
